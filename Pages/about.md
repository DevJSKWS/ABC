Page: About Us (/about)

ABC Web Services is a division of ABC Software, an Ahmedabad based Software and Web Development Company established in 1998. ABC Software is a reputed and one of the fastest growing Software Company in Ahmedabad with having clients in more than 30 countries.

As the business of ABC Software is constantly growing in International Market especially with IT Outsourcing and Offshore Development Services, a separate division was started to serve the local market.

The purpose and objective of ABC Web Services is to use the skills and experience gained by ABC Software in International Market to provide less expensive web site services to our local clients.

ABC Web Services is actively involved in developing static and dynamic web sites for local businesses. Many of local businesses are not much aware of the benefits of web sites, and if they are aware of that, they do not have the knowledge and resources to develop their own web sites. Having a website is technically difficult considering the process of domain name registration, web hosting, and web design and development.

Therefore, with the purpose of simplifying this for our local clients, we have come up with the packages which include all the above aspects and steps towards having your own website. From the business point of view, the process is as simple as publishing an advertisement in local Yellow Pages.

We are providing several packages for website development to suit the requirements and budgets of the businesses. Our prices are affordable to very small businesses as well as the reputed companies.

The aim of ABC Web Services is to provide the web development services of International Quality at the Indian Prices!!!
